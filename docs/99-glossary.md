# Glossary

- **Blueprint:** one-page representation of the problem, users, outcomes, scope, constraints, success criteria, risks, and assumptions.
- **Gate:** binary pass/fail checkpoint.
- **Element card:** specification and interface contract for one small deliverable element.
- **Assumption log:** explicit record of unverified statements and their verification owners.
- **Open-questions log:** questions without answers, each assigned to an owner and due date.
- **Execution Readiness Pack:** the thirteen records required before actual execution.
- **Interface contract:** the inputs, outputs, format, and connection behavior between elements.
